## Top risks at fan-out

The initial highest-priority risks are R-01/R-02 (split authority/runtime), R-03 (data truth), R-05/R-06/R-07 (immutability and durable correctness), R-08/R-10 (fake/unstructured success), R-16/R-19 (product contract/fake drift), and R-23/R-24 (parallel merge/evidence integrity). Each has a named early gate; none should be deferred solely to the final slice.

## Risk review cadence

- Bootstrap: confirm triggers/owners and external prerequisites.
- Every cross-plan gate: close, re-score, or add risks based on fresh evidence.
- Before release candidate: all High-impact risks need a passing prevention/detection test or explicit **BLOCKED** state.
- After each real slice: update only from persisted evidence; a rerun does not erase the failed-run record.
- Final report: list residual risks and post-Roadmap work separately from mandatory acceptance.
